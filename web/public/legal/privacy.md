## Personal Data We Collect

**Account data.** When you register, we collect your email address, your name and the account type you choose (individual, business or company). For business and company accounts we may also collect your organisation's name and details. We store your password only in hashed form and never see it in plain text. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

**Session and security data.** When you sign in, we set a session cookie so you stay signed in. Like any web service, our servers and network provider process technical data such as IP address, browser type, request timestamps and error information. We use it to deliver the service, keep it secure and investigate problems.

**AI assistant conversations.** If you use the AI assistant, we store the messages you send, the responses it generates, and any actions you confirm in a conversation, so you can come back to your conversation history.

**Feedback.** If you rate an assistant response or send us feedback, we store that feedback together with the related conversation.

**Content you submit.** If you submit or edit charger specification data as a manufacturer or contributor, we store that content and your account as its author. The registry is open data, so submitted specification content is publicly visible.

**Billing data (once paid plans are available).** If you subscribe to a paid plan, we will process billing details such as billing name, address, VAT number and invoice history. Card or other payment details will be handled directly by our payment provider and will not be stored by us.

We do not run advertising or third-party analytics/tracking scripts, and we do not sell your personal data.

## The AI Assistant

The AI assistant is an automated system, not a human. When you send a message, its content is sent to our AI provider (Google) to generate a response. The provider processes it on our behalf and under our instructions, and may not use it to train its models.

AI responses can be inaccurate or incomplete. Please do not enter sensitive personal data (for example health information, financial details or other people's personal data) into the assistant.

We do not use the assistant, or any other part of the service, to make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects (GDPR Art. 22).

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out, or up to 24 hours | Us |

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erase** your data. You can delete your account at any time in your account settings
- **Restrict** our processing of your data in certain cases
- **Data portability**: receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing based on our legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing that happened before

To exercise these rights, email **[privacy@chargepi.cc]**. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Slovenia this is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec), Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, [www.ip-rs.si](https://www.ip-rs.si). You may also contact the authority in your own EU country.

## Security

No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the supervisory authority as required by law.
