This Privacy Policy explains how OECS Hub ("we", "our", "the service") handles information when you use this site.

OECS Hub is an open, vendor-neutral registry for EV charger specifications. This policy is a general, good-faith description of our data practices and is provided for transparency — it is not a substitute for legal advice.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information.** If you create an account, authentication is handled by Ory Kratos. We store the information you provide during registration (such as your email address) and the credentials needed to authenticate you. We do not see or store your raw password.
- **Session data.** Signing in creates a session, typically stored via a cookie, so you stay signed in between visits.
- **Content you submit.** If you submit or edit charger specification data as a manufacturer or contributor, that content is stored in the registry and may be publicly visible, since the registry itself is open data.

We do not run advertising or third-party analytics/tracking scripts on this site.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

- Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in
- Operate and improve the registry (e.g. attributing submitted charger data)
- Communicate with you about your account when necessary (e.g. password resets, verification)

## Data Storage & Security

Account and session data is stored using Ory Kratos. Registry data (manufacturers, chargers, specifications) is stored in our backend databases. We take reasonable measures to protect this data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Third-Party Services

- **Ory Kratos** — used for authentication and identity management.
- **GitHub** — the OECS specification and OECS Hub source code are hosted publicly on GitHub; links to GitHub in this app are external and governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies necessary for authentication and session management. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make such a request, please contact us via the channel below.

## Children's Privacy

OECS Hub is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact
